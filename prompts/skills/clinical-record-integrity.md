# Skill: Clinical record integrity

- Distinguish draft, signed, closed and voided clinical states.
- A signed record is immutable; corrections become addenda.
- The veterinarian is responsible for diagnosis and treatment decisions.
- Dose helpers must show units, arithmetic, inputs and require clinician confirmation.
- Distinguish prescribed, dispensed and administered products.
- Keep attachments linked to patient and encounter with access control.
- Include author, timestamp and branch in clinical records.
