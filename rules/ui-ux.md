# UI/UX Rules
- Follow `design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `design/tokens.json`.
- Turkish-first language; use consistent “Hasta” and “Hasta sahibi” terminology.
- Every screen has loading, empty, error, success and unauthorized states.
- Forms show inline validation and preserve safe drafts.
- Tables support search/filter/sort/pagination when appropriate.
- Keep service finalization review explicit: clinical lines, products, lots, qty, totals.
- Make draft/synced/finalized states unmistakable.
- Avoid color-only status, emoji icons, excessive gradients and decorative cards.
- Ensure keyboard support, focus states, screen-reader labels and minimum touch targets.
